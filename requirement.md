# TECHASCENT - MACHINE TEST: Superhero Help Portal Requirements

**Start Date:** 11 September 2026
**Submission Deadline:** 17 September 2026
**Target Audience:** Students of Jyothi College of Engineering

## The Challenge
Design and develop a website for an original superhero where people can come and ask for help. 
The superhero must be completely original, featuring a unique name, personality, story, powers, mission, and visual identity. The website should make visitors feel like they are directly interacting with this superhero.

---

## Core Requirements

### 1. An Attractive Superhero Website
- **Content:** Must include the superhero's name, identity, hero image/illustration, origin story, powers and abilities, mission, personality, and purpose.
- **Call-to-Action (CTA):** Must include a compelling CTA encouraging visitors to reach out and ask for help.
- **Design Quality:** Must not be a basic landing page. Requires high-quality overall design, animations, interactions, storytelling, and an immersive user experience that makes the superhero feel like a real character.

### 2. Superhero Chatbot
- **Trigger:** The superhero should appear through a chatbot or conversational interface as soon as someone enters the website.
- **Data Collection:** Through natural conversation, the chatbot must collect:
  - Name
  - Age
  - Location
  - Email address
  - Any other relevant basic information
- **Grievance Submission:** After collecting basic info, the chatbot should ask how it can help (e.g., *"So... tell me. How can I help you?"*). The visitor should then be able to describe their grievance, problem, request, or situation.
- **UX Goal:** The interaction should feel engaging and personalized, avoiding the feel of a traditional web form.

### 3. Automatic Email Notification
- **Trigger:** Upon submission of the grievance/request.
- **Action:** System automatically sends an email to the developer's (candidate's) personal email address.
- **Email Content:**
  - Subject line (e.g., *Someone Needs Your Help!*)
  - Visitor's name
  - Age
  - Location
  - Email address
  - Grievance / Request details
  - Date and time of submission
- **Constraint:** Must be generated automatically; no manual checking of the website should be required.

### 4. Hosting & Deployment
- The completed project must be hosted and accessible via a public URL.
- The evaluation team must be able to interact with the live superhero portal and test the complete end-to-end experience.

### 5. Creativity & Innovation
- **Originality:** Do not copy an existing superhero. The character must be highly original.
- **Innovation:** Extra features and creative ideas that improve the experience (making it more useful, interactive, or memorable) will receive additional consideration.

---

## Technical Constraints & Freedom
- **Technology Stack:** Completely open. You are free to choose your own frameworks, libraries, APIs, and design styles.
- **Hosting Platform:** Any suitable deployment platform.
- **Goal:** Demonstrate the ability to think, design, build, integrate, deploy, and deliver a complete, working digital product.

---

## Evaluation Criteria
1. **Speed of Completion:** Efficiency in building and deploying before the deadline.
2. **Look & Feel:** Attractiveness, modern design, responsiveness, engagement, and overall polish.
3. **Functionality:** Chatbot interaction, information collection, grievance submission, automatic email notifications, responsive behavior, and overall working mechanics.
